## 1. Brand Overview & Personality

### Mission Statement
Simplifying facility management through intelligent automation, data-driven insights, and continuous improvement. We empower facility managers and enterprises to optimize operations, reduce costs, and deliver exceptional tenant experiences.

### Brand Essence
**Kaizen** (改善) — The philosophy of continuous improvement — is at the heart of our brand. We combine this principle with cutting-edge AI technology to create facility management solutions that evolve, learn, and improve over time.

### Brand Personality

**Primary Attributes:**
- **Intelligent & Innovative** — AI-powered, predictive, forward-thinking
- **Reliable & Trustworthy** — 99.9% uptime, enterprise-grade security, ISO certified
- **Efficient & Streamlined** — Automation-first, reducing complexity
- **Professional & Expert** — Industry-leading, data-driven, technically competent
- **Approachable & Supportive** — 24/7 support, user-centric design

### Tone of Voice

**Do:**
- Speak with authority and technical confidence
- Use clear, jargon-free language when addressing diverse audiences
- Emphasize measurable outcomes and efficiency gains
- Convey innovation through forward-looking language
- Be helpful and solution-oriented

**Don't:**
- Use overly casual or playful language
- Over-complicate with excessive technical jargon
- Make unsubstantiated claims
- Use fear-based messaging
- Adopt an aggressive sales tone

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Automate maintenance workflows and reduce downtime by 40%"
- ✅ "Real-time insights that drive smarter facility decisions"
- ❌ "Say goodbye to facility headaches forever!"
- ❌ "Revolutionary game-changer that will blow your mind"

### Target Audience

**Primary:**
- Facility Managers at enterprise organizations
- Property Management companies (CBRE, JLL, Colliers, Cushman & Wakefield)
- Corporate real estate teams
- Operations directors at commercial properties

**Secondary:**
- IT decision-makers evaluating CAFM/CMMS systems
- Procurement teams
- Building owners and investors
- Residential society managers

**Audience Needs:**
- Operational efficiency and cost reduction
- Data visibility and reporting
- Compliance and safety management
- Scalable, enterprise-grade solutions
- Integration with existing systems (ERP, CRM, BMS, IoT)

## 3. Typography System

### Font Families

**Primary Typeface: Inter**
- **Usage:** All digital interfaces, web applications, dashboards, mobile apps
- **Rationale:** Inter is specifically designed for UI. Excellent readability at small sizes, extensive weight range, optimized for screens
- **Source:** Google Fonts or self-hosted
- **Fallback:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`

**Secondary Typeface: IBM Plex Sans**
- **Usage:** Marketing materials, presentations, documentation, printed materials
- **Rationale:** Professional, slightly warmer than Inter, excellent for long-form content
- **Source:** Google Fonts or self-hosted
- **Fallback:** `"Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`

**Monospace Typeface: JetBrains Mono**
- **Usage:** Code snippets, API documentation, technical specifications, data tables
- **Rationale:** Designed for developers, excellent ligature support, clear differentiation
- **Source:** Google Fonts or self-hosted

### Typography Guidelines

**Hierarchy Rules:**
- Never skip heading levels (don't jump from H1 to H3)
- Maintain consistent vertical rhythm using 8px base grid
- Limit to 3 weights per interface (Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600)
- Reserve Bold (700) for emphasis within text, not headings

**Readability:**
- Body text should never exceed 75 characters per line (optimal: 50-60)
- Minimum font size for body text: 14px desktop, 16px mobile
- Always provide sufficient contrast (WCAG AA minimum)
- Use sentence case for buttons and labels, not ALL CAPS (except small tertiary buttons)

**Emphasis & Styling:**
- Bold: Use Medium (500) or SemiBold (600) weight, never underline
- Italics: Use for subtle emphasis or quotes (avoid in UI labels)
- Links: Primary Blue color + medium weight + subtle underline on hover
- All caps: Only for very small labels (12px) where hierarchy is clear

## 4. Logo Usage Guidelines

### Logo Variations

**Primary Logo**
- Full horizontal wordmark with icon
- Preferred usage for all materials
- Icon represents continuous flow and automation (abstract kaizen cycle)

**Icon-Only Logo**
- Used when space is constrained (< 120px width)
- App icons, social media avatars, favicons
- Maintains brand recognition without wordmark

**Monochrome Versions**
- White logo on dark backgrounds
- Deep Navy logo on light backgrounds
- Never use gray logos; always full contrast

### Clear Space & Minimum Sizes

**Clear Space:**
- Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "K" in "Kaizen" on all sides
- No text, graphics, or other elements should enter this protected zone
- Clear space scales proportionally with logo size

**Minimum Sizes:**
- Digital: 120px width (horizontal logo), 32px height (icon only)
- Print: 0.75 inches width (horizontal logo), 0.25 inches (icon only)
- Never scale below these dimensions to ensure legibility

### Background Usage Rules

**Preferred Backgrounds:**
- Pure White (#FFFFFF) with Deep Navy logo
- Deep Navy (#1A2B3C) with White logo
- Light Gray (#F3F4F6) with Deep Navy logo

**Acceptable Backgrounds:**
- Primary Blue (#0066CC) with White logo
- Photographs/Images: Use white logo with dark gradient overlay (opacity 0.7+)

**Never Use:**
- Logo on Alert Orange, Critical Red, or low-contrast colors
- Logo on busy patterns without sufficient contrast overlay
- Logo smaller than minimum size specifications
- Stretched, rotated, or distorted logo
- Logo with drop shadows or effects (except subtle shadow on photos if needed)

### Visual Style Rules

**Logo Modifications — NEVER:**
- Change logo colors beyond approved variations
- Add effects (gradients, shadows, glows, outlines)
- Rotate, skew, or stretch the logo
- Rearrange elements or alter spacing
- Place logo on colors that fail contrast requirements
- Recreate or redraw the logo
- Use outdated logo versions

**Logo Modifications — ACCEPTABLE:**
- Scale uniformly (maintaining aspect ratio)
- Switch between approved color variations based on background
- Use icon-only version in space-constrained scenarios
- Add subtle shadow when placing white logo on photographs (for legibility)

**File Formats:**
- Vector: SVG (web), EPS/PDF (print) — preferred for all scalable uses
- Raster: PNG with transparency (digital), JPEG (photos with logo overlay)
- Minimum resolution for raster: 300 DPI for print, 144 DPI (2x) for retina displays

## 5. Practical Layout Rules

### Spacing System (8px Base Grid)

All spacing follows an 8px base unit for consistent rhythm and alignment.

**Spacing Tokens:**
XXS: 4px   (0.25rem)  — Micro spacing, icon-text gaps
XS:  8px   (0.5rem)   — Tight spacing, inline elements
SM:  12px  (0.75rem)  — Compact spacing, form field groups
MD:  16px  (1rem)     — Default spacing, card padding
LG:  24px  (1.5rem)   — Section spacing, component gaps
XL:  32px  (2rem)     — Major section spacing
2XL: 48px  (3rem)     — Hero sections, page margins
3XL: 64px  (4rem)     — Large breakpoints, feature sections
4XL: 96px  (6rem)     — Maximum spacing, landing page sections

**Application Guidelines:**
- Card padding: MD (16px) for compact cards, LG (24px) for standard cards
- Section margins: XL (32px) between sections, 2XL (48px) for hero sections
- Form field spacing: SM (12px) between label and input, MD (16px) between fields
- Button padding: XS (8px) vertical, MD (16px) horizontal for default buttons
- Icon-text spacing: XXS (4px) for inline, XS (8px) for standard

### Border Radius (Corner Radius)

**Roundness Tokens:**
None:   0px     — Data tables, strict layouts, enterprise dashboards
SM:     4px     — Buttons, inputs, tags, badges, small components
MD:     8px     — Cards, modals, dropdowns, standard components
LG:     12px    — Hero cards, feature sections, large containers
XL:     16px    — Decorative elements, marketing sections
Full:   9999px  — Pills, circular avatars, toggle switches

**Usage:**
- Input fields: SM (4px)
- Buttons: SM (4px) for standard, MD (8px) for large CTAs
- Cards: MD (8px) for dashboard cards, LG (12px) for landing page cards
- Modals/Overlays: MD (8px)
- Images: MD (8px) standard, LG (12px) for hero images
- Avatars: Full (9999px) for circular avatars

### Layout Grid System

**Container Widths:**
Mobile:      100% with 16px padding
Tablet:      768px max-width, 24px padding
Desktop:     1280px max-width, 32px padding
Wide:        1440px max-width, 40px padding

**Column Grid:**
- 12-column grid for desktop
- 8-column grid for tablet
- 4-column grid for mobile
- Gutter: 24px (1.5rem)

### Component Geometry

**Buttons:**
Small:   Height 32px, Padding 8px 12px, Font 12px
Default: Height 40px, Padding 10px 16px, Font 14px
Large:   Height 48px, Padding 12px 24px, Font 16px

**Input Fields:**
Default: Height 40px, Padding 10px 12px, Border 1px
Large:   Height 48px, Padding 12px 16px, Border 1px

**Cards:**
Compact:  Padding 16px, Border-radius 8px
Standard: Padding 24px, Border-radius 8px
Feature:  Padding 32px, Border-radius 12px

### Elevation & Shadows

**Shadow Tokens:**
None:   none
SM:     0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)
— Subtle hover states, slight elevation
MD:     0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1),
0 2px 4px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)
— Cards, dropdowns, standard elevation
LG:     0 10px 15px -3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1),
0 4px 6px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)
— Modals, popovers, raised components
XL:     0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1),
0 10px 10px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)
— Large modals, critical floating elements

**Usage:**
- Cards: MD shadow on white backgrounds, SM on gray backgrounds
- Dropdowns/Menus: MD shadow
- Modals: LG shadow with overlay
- Hover states: Transition from current shadow to one level higher
- Avoid shadows on shadows (don't stack elevated components)

### Responsive Breakpoints
Mobile:       < 640px
Tablet:       640px - 1024px
Desktop:      1024px - 1440px
Wide:         > 1440px

**Responsive Guidelines:**
- Mobile-first approach: Design for smallest screen, enhance upward
- Touch targets: Minimum 44x44px on mobile
- Font sizes: Scale down headings by 20-30% on mobile
- Spacing: Reduce by 25-50% on mobile (e.g., 32px → 16px)
- Navigation: Hamburger menu below 1024px

## Design System Implementation Notes

### Priority Principles

1. **Consistency over creativity** — Use established patterns
2. **Clarity over cleverness** — Information hierarchy is paramount
3. **Efficiency over aesthetics** — Users need fast, reliable tools
4. **Accessibility is mandatory** — WCAG 2.1 AA minimum standard
5. **Mobile-responsive by default** — Never desktop-only

### Component Hierarchy

**Critical UI Elements (Highest Priority):**
- Data tables and dashboards
- Forms and inputs (work order creation, asset management)
- Status indicators and alerts
- Navigation and wayfinding

**Supporting Elements:**
- Charts and data visualizations
- Modal dialogs
- Empty states and loading states
- Error messages and validation

**Marketing/Promotional Elements:**
- Landing pages
- Feature showcases
- Testimonials
- Blog posts

### Accessibility Requirements

- **Color:** Never use color alone to convey information
- **Contrast:** Minimum 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and UI elements
- **Focus States:** Visible keyboard focus indicators on all interactive elements
- **Alt Text:** All images must have descriptive alternative text
- **Form Labels:** Every input must have an associated label
- **ARIA:** Proper ARIA attributes for complex components
- **Testing:** Regular testing with screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver)
